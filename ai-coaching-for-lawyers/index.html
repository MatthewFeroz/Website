What Is AI Coaching for Lawyers?

AI coaching for lawyers is private, hands-on training that teaches attorneys how to use artificial intelligence tools — like ChatGPT, Claude, and legal AI assistants — to do high-value legal work faster and more accurately. Instead of generic online courses, you get personalized 1:1 sessions built around your real cases, your practice area, and your firm's workflows.

As a software engineer with four years of hands-on AI experience, I translate complex AI concepts into practical, secure workflows that fit the realities of legal work. From legal research and case summaries to contract drafting, deposition prep, and discovery review, you'll learn how to delegate the repetitive work to AI while keeping your professional judgment firmly in control.
